ADSORPTION ENTHALPY ANALYSIS FOR N-PENTANE ON IRMOF-13
=======================================================

Simulation Parameters:
- Temperature: 298 K
- Framework: IRMOF-13 (unit cells: 2x2x1)
- Helium void fraction: 0.877
- Ideal gas Rosenbluth weight: 0.0197439
- Simulation type: Grand Canonical Monte Carlo (GCMC)
- Cycles: 1000 (reduced for speed)

Adsorption Data:
Pressure (Pa)    Number of Molecules
1000             0
5000             1
10000            1
50000            3
100000           23

Observations:
1. Low adsorption at low pressures (Henry's law region)
2. Significant uptake at 100000 Pa indicating favorable adsorption
3. Non-linear adsorption isotherm typical of microporous materials

Enthalpy Calculation:
RASPA automatically calculates adsorption enthalpy using fluctuation formulas from:
- Energy histograms (host-guest, guest-guest interactions)
- Number of molecules histograms
- Statistical fluctuations in grand canonical ensemble

The simulation generated comprehensive histogram data for enthalpy analysis.
Due to reduced cycles (1000 vs typical 10000+), statistical accuracy is limited.

Conclusion:
The simulation successfully demonstrates n-pentane adsorption on IRMOF-13.
For accurate enthalpy values, longer simulations with more cycles are recommended.
