For comparing adsorption enthalpies of different molecules on a framework using RASPA: Use Monte Carlo simulation with CreateNumberOfMolecules 1 (single molecule insertion), external pressure 0.0 for infinite dilution, rigid framework required. The enthalpy of adsorption is calculated as ΔH = ⟨U_hg⟩ - ⟨U_h⟩ - ⟨U_g⟩ - RT, where the key output is 'Total energy' giving ⟨U_hg⟩ directly. For rigid frameworks and simple molecules: ΔH = (Total_energy - T) × R_gas_constant. Each molecule requires separate simulation runs with consistent temperature and framework conditions.
