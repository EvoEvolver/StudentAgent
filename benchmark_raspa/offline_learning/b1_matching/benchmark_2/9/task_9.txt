Henry coefficient simulations in RASPA require MonteCarlo simulation type with Widom particle insertion method. Key parameters include: WidomProbability 1.0, CreateNumberOfMolecules 0 for each component, and IdealGasRosenbluthWeight for molecules where known. The helium void fraction must be specified for the framework. Temperature is typically 298K and pressure 1e5 Pa for standard conditions.
