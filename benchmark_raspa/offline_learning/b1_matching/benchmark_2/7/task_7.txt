The simulation uses Widom insertion probe moves without actually inserting molecules, only computing energy at random positions.

Extract 'Average Widom Rosenbluth factor' from output - this IS the IdealGasRosenbluthWeight needed for main Henry coefficient simulation. Must be calculated at same temperature as main simulation.
