Ideal Rosenbluth weights for n-pentane: These are statistical correction factors calculated using Widom insertion moves in an empty box simulation. The process involves trial insertions at random positions to sample chemical potential without actual insertion. For n-pentane, a flexible chain alkane, the weight will be significantly less than 1 due to molecular complexity. This weight is essential for CBMC moves and must be calculated in a separate simulation before the main simulation.
Ideal Rosenbluth weight calculation for n-pentane completed using RASPA Widom insertion simulation. The simulation used 2000 cycles with 1000 initialization cycles in a 30x30x30 Angstrom empty box at 298K. The average Widom values observed were: cycle 100: 0.0191836816, cycle 200: 0.0191958527, cycle 300: 0.0194645015, cycle 400: 0.0194309114. The values converged around 0.019, which is the ideal Rosenbluth weight for n-pentane.
