Ideal Rosenbluth weights calculation in RASPA: Uses Widom particle insertion method in empty box (30x30x30 Angstrom), SimulationType MonteCarlo, WidomProbability 1.0, CreateNumberOfMolecules 0, Temperature specified, NumberOfCycles 20000. Output gives 'Average Widom Rosenbluth factor' which is the IdealGasRosenbluthWeight needed for CBMC moves. Essential prerequisite for n-hexane and n-pentane simulations.
